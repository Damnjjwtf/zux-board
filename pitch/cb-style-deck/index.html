Our script — one set of words, six looks

Forget the landmarks. The real story of a city is told through its food — in the neighborhoods, at the market stalls, in the small kitchens you’d never find on your own. We take you there — to meet the masters. The unsung heroes who’ve made one thing, perfectly, their whole lives. You taste their work. You hear their stories. And a tradition stays alive. Culinary Backstreets. Food walks, led by locals, in more than 25 cities. Travel, with bite.

Why one script? So the look is the only variable. Same words, same voice, same 30 seconds — you’re judging pure visual style.

Ground rules we held: All illustration / animation. Nothing fakes a real tour, guide or customer. AI does the drawing; the words are ours, written for this test — a stand-in for your team’s copy.

The six looks

Six finished spots. Same script, same voice — the only thing that changes is the look.

1 · Sketchbook

Pencil, ink and watercolor — a traveler’s notebook come to life. The loosest hand in the set, and the most human; every frame feels drawn on the spot.

2 · Poster

Two-color screen-print. Bold, cheap to remake, reads at a glance in a feed.

3 · Vintage photo

1970s snapshot warmth. The boundary case: it fakes documentary, so its honest use is as a grade over CB’s real archive — never generated scenes.

4 · Manga

Black-and-white ink; the only color is the burgundy end card. Strongest likeness of the set — a possible Tokyo / Osaka variant.

5 · Cut-paper

Torn-paper stop-motion. The most handmade of the set — the master is a paper design, so he never drifts.

6 · Clay

Claymation miniatures. Tactile and unmistakably handmade; a clay master stays the same man in every shot.

The system

The spine never changes. The look, the stall, the master, the beats, the closing line — that’s the signature. A stranger should know it’s Culinary Backstreets in one second.

Only two things swap. Who’s traveling, and what’s getting in their way — the frazzled parent, the lost solo traveler, the group that can’t agree. Each one drops into the same bones.

The rest is spec, not taste. Same opening beat, same type, same burgundy end card, same length. A new ad is assembled in days, not invented from scratch.

Every ad is named for its swaps. So when the numbers come back, we know exactly which traveler — and which problem — earned their keep.
