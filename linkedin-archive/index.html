What LinkedIn is taking out

Some things stay in the file: names, profile links, and the dates you connected.

Some things go: work email addresses, companies, and job titles.

LinkedIn has not said the exact day. Do it by November 30 and you are safe.

Why it matters: the company and job parts are what turn a plain list of names into a map of where your people work now.

How to get your files

There are two files. The first takes two minutes to ask for. The second takes one minute.

File one: the big one

On a computer, signed in to LinkedIn:

  1. Click your photo at the top, then Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click Data privacy, then Download your data.
  3. Pick the larger archive, the one with everything in it.
  4. Click Request archive.
LinkedIn needs about a day to get it ready. Then they email you a link. The link only works for 72 hours, so download the file as soon as the email comes. Keep it somewhere safe.

File two: how your posts did

This file shows how many people saw your posts and liked them. It is not part of the big file. Here is how to get it:

  1. Go to your own profile page.
  2. Find the box called Analytics. Click Show all analytics.
  3. Click Posts.
  4. Set the time to Past 365 days.
  5. Click Export, at the top right. This file downloads right away.
